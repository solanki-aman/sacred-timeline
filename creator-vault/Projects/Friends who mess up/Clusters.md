# Clusters

Each line is one cluster for this project: **name**: what belongs in it. Edit here or on the moment map.

- **Bravado**: a man speaking with total confidence, boasting or showing off
- **The crew**: a group of friends together, joking and egging each other on
- **Cracks show**: someone embarrassed, flustered, caught out or losing their cool
- **Friction**: people annoyed with each other, arguing or bickering
- **Gatekeepers**: staff or officials dealing with the group, like a receptionist or clerk
